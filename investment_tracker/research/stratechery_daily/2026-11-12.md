## [An Interview With Roblox CEO David Baszucki About Aggregating Play](https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-roblox-ceo-david-baszucki-about-aggregating-play/) (2026-11-12, score 16.8, free)

Roblox is pursuing that with an AI game-creation harness ("Roblox Build"), a recommendation engine, a "Universal Player" that ships Roblox games beyond its own app, and advertising. Roblox is not in the tracker universe.

- **Alphabet:** Baszucki says that even if AI could rebuild YouTube, "people are still going to go to YouTube to watch video." He also lists the Google Play Store as one place standalone Roblox-built games could ship. **Inference:** this backs the view that AI raises the value of incumbent distribution platforms (YouTube, Play) rather than eroding them. It is a framing point for GOOGL, not data.
