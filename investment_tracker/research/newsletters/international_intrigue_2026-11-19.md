## What matters for the tracker
1. **Suppliers are funding their customers (H1.C5).** **Inference:** NVIDIA, TSMC and Micron, plus Google, are buying equity in a big compute buyer at the IPO price. That is the vendor-financing loop H1.C5 tracks through Broadcom's rack-lease backstop, now in equity form. The IPO also gives the market a listed AI-lab stock to compare with the neoclouds.
2. **Memory is still tight (H1.T3).** A console maker guiding to memory prices doubling again by late 2027 supports the H1 bottleneck test: memory revenue growth holding.
3. **NVDA is not trading on its numbers.** It fell after a beat and is down ~25% since Nov 4. **Inference:** governance and export policy are setting the price, not demand. That matches the newsletter's Nov 18 take.
