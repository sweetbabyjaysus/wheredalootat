Ben Thompson's "Google Capital Company" thesis

In his June essay, Search is See's Candies (a cash machine needing little capital) and Cloud is BNSF Railway (capital-hungry, lower margin, potentially much bigger profits). His own test was whether Google would also issue a lot of debt.

Inference

The operating half of the thesis is on track. The return-on-capital half is not proven yet: free cash flow is negative and Q3 needed about $41bn of outside money. The 29 Oct drop reads as the market starting to price Google as the railway rather than the candy shop.

Broadcom and Google's chips

  • Broadcom designs and supplies Google's TPUs, plus networking for its AI racks, through up to 2031 (8-K, 6 Apr). Anthropic gets ~3.5 GW of TPU compute via Broadcom from 2027, "dependent on Anthropic's continued commercial success".
  • Broadcom guarantees up to ~$43bn of the value of AI racks leased to an unnamed customer (8-K, 27 Oct). This is supplier-side credit risk, the same shape as Nvidia's resale-value guarantees.
  • The stocks don't move together much (1-year correlation 0.29). When Google fell on its own spending, Broadcom rose: 28 Oct −11.5% vs +0.8%; 22 Jul −8.5% vs +1.5%. Inference: Broadcom trades on Google's capex, not Google's profits.
  • Broadcom itself: revenue +86%, operating margin 54%, net debt/EBITDA 0.8x, 17.7x forward earnings, next results 9 Dec.
